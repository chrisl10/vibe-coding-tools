# Prepare the contractor handoff packet

Prepare one source-linked packet with assignment boundaries, setup, interfaces, acceptance, ownership and operational exit conditions.

Before starting, confirm repository/revision, assignment goal, authorized audience/output path, contractor/receiver identity if supplied, access boundaries and test budget. Use existing Knowledge and Contract Writing owners through the selected-owner resolver. No dispatch capability or account access is implied by source presence.

1. Use the [packet worksheet](template.md). Record exact in-scope/excluded paths, deliverables, source identity, existing shared owners, known risks and unresolved decisions. Keep unknown owner or authority evidence pending.
2. Add the contributor setup and architecture orientation from current source. Record runtime/toolchain, cwd, existing setup/check commands, expected outputs and actual results. Access entries contain system/role/variable names and owner-provided steps only, never secret values. Missing access or a clean runner leaves the dependent setup step UNEXECUTED.
3. Inventory engineering interfaces with exact code/schema locators and provider/consumer parties. Resolve Core Contract Writing for shared agreement gaps or revisions. Pin each applicable CTR path, revision, status and actual acceptance source. Missing, Draft, disputed or stale terms block dependent work. Do not accept a Draft merely to complete a packet. If no CTR applies, state why from actual boundary evidence.
4. Link each observable assignment acceptance criterion to its responsible implementer, independent reviewer/acceptor, required check, candidate identity and evidence state. Contract term acceptance and whole-assignment delivery acceptance are separate records. Legal/commercial terms and rights remain pending with their actual reviewers; do not infer them from a CTR or contributor packet.
5. Record operational ownership for support, release, recovery and access handback. Define assignment exit: final source/artifact identity, outstanding defects/risks, accepted or blocked criteria, operating notes and the owner of any later access-revocation or provider action. Prepare those actions; do not perform them through the packet.
6. Ask the receiver to acknowledge the exact packet revision, understood scope, accepted responsibility and unresolved questions when communication is separately authorized. A local draft acknowledgment request is not a send or an acknowledgment receipt. Keep receiver acknowledgment pending until actual source evidence identifies the receiver, date, revision and response. No acknowledgment silently approves deploy, spend or contract terms.
7. Check every packet dimension is complete or explicitly blocked; verify source links and commands against the pinned source. Record actual setup and check results separately from structural packet checks. Deliver `contractor-handoff.md` alongside contributor instructions and setup proof.

Grounding: current project digest. [Cases](cases.md) are synthetic and NOT_RUN.
