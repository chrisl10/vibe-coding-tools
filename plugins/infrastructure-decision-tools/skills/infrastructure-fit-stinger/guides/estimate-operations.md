# Hand off costs and operator work

1. Resolve the actual selected Operating Cost owner. Supply the same low/base/high workload to every candidate, plus dated rates, currency/region/plan, charge units, included usage/floors and assumptions.
2. Include compute, DB, storage, egress, backup retention, logs/alerts, support, private-network features and expected growth. Distinguish customer-paid service expenses from a valuation of operator hours.
3. Ask Operating Cost for its existing cost model and proof. Do not duplicate its calculator here or invent totals when rates are missing. Missing capability or rates makes cost unresolved; explain the exact handoff needed.
4. Compare viable topologies using its returned assumptions and uncertainty. A lower service invoice cannot establish lower total cost if operator work is omitted.
5. Add recovery/exit labor and data-export/egress assumptions. Deliver one recommendation with visible budget feasibility or a stated unresolved budget constraint.

Grounding: architectural cost/operations balance AWS framework; actual compute models Cloud Run, Vercel. The Operating Cost boundary and input contract come from the approved topic.
