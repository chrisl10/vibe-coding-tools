# Infrastructure fit digest

Fresh retrieval October 7, 2026; sources are current public official documentation, with publication dates recorded only when exposed. Provider overviews establish service shape, not price, regional eligibility, workload throughput or an application's production readiness. No service was provisioned or benchmarked.

## Limits and decisions

Vultr compute retrieval returned `HTTP Error 404: Not Found`; no capability, price, region or performance assertion about that track is supported by this archive. It stays an unqualified alternative pending an accessible applicable official source.

This envelope deliberately does not fix numeric provider rates or limits into a selection rule. Runtime qualification retrieves current plan/region/runtime-specific tables and records their date. AWS-expensive and GCP-complicated are user concerns to test against the normalized workload, required services and operator skill, not universal factual conclusions.

Interview fields, exclusion rules, responsibility tables, decisions and scenarios below are approved topic workflow design, not provider guarantees. Cost math belongs to the selected Operating Cost specialist. No duplicate calculator or costless inference is supplied. A source archive and a decision worksheet cannot prove latency, availability, compliance or a restore test.
