# Prepare the cost envelope
Use the [worksheet](../references/templates/fit-worksheet.md) and plans archive. This guide prepares inputs; selected Operating Cost owns low/base/high arithmetic.

1. Identify account/workspace, plan, region, currency, billing period and observation date without credentials or account actions. Distinguish container from VM primitive; their rate tables differ.
2. Collect low/base/high measured or explicit assumed RAM-time, CPU-time, outbound GB, used volume GB-time, incremental backup storage, and replica/service counts. Request and user counts do not establish these meters. Preserve GB versus GiB and minute versus month units. Volumes, backups
3. Attach dated rate evidence to every meter. Treat the ordinary Hobby/Pro included usage and fee as one workspace minimum over aggregate eligible usage; do not duplicate a credit per project/service or charge the included amount twice. Separate true promotional/prepaid pools only with actual eligible scope and ordering proof. The digest documents why the generic model cannot silently assume a different credit order.
4. Reconcile unknown backup/egress/tax/support charges, plan maximums, payment/trial effects, and any partial cycle treatment. Unknown is unpriced, never zero. A hard usage limit can stop workloads; do not propose or enable a limit as though it preserves availability. Billing FAQ
5. Resolve Operating Cost by catalog source root during source work or trusted Core selected-plugin resolution when installed. Supply the meter table and fee interpretation. If unavailable, report totals unavailable and the precise owner gap. Never create a second calculator.

Output provider cash separately from paid labor and unpaid time valuation. Do not claim an actual invoice match, change pricing controls or provide a live price from this archive. Any real estimate refreshes evidence at the time of the task.
