## Scope and limits

The ledger's statuses, evidence classes, blocking/dependency rules and exclusion recipes are the approved [topic contract](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/infrastructure-decision-tools/skills/launch-assets-stinger/references/research/topic.md), not vendor statements. Account ownership, secret provisioning, spending authority and release approval require actual task authority and evidence. Public docs do not establish any user's account state.

No universal approval duration is supported here. Record lead time as unknown unless the provider publishes an applicable duration or the user supplies a dated measured record. Vendor estimates are not guarantees. No full cloud cost model or market comparison is attempted. The current sources carry varying publication dates; a fresh capture is not a claim every page was published in 2026.
