# Worked truth and false-proof cases

All companies, counts, rates and prices below are hypothetical fixtures, not current market facts.

## Valid provisional worksheet

A single-location employer segment is counted as establishments; the analyst explicitly leaves firm-level buying-account conversion unresolved. Supplied hypothetical scenario A instead uses 1,000 distinct verified account records, 0.20 reach, 0.10 conditional conversion, capacity 12 and USD 1,200 annual price. Reached = 200, uncapped expected served = 20, capacity-capped served = 12, annualized scenario revenue = USD 14,400. The twelve-month horizon is stated. No profit, likelihood or cash receipt forecast follows. Conversion remains assumed, and the output is provisional until calibrated.

## False proof rejected

A vendor pricing page says its search is 30% faster. The claim is vendor_claim with matched-test proof unknown. Tagging it observed with only a vendor source fails the helper. Adding an observation tag alone can satisfy schema but still does not prove a test took place; the human reviewer requires the actual benchmark method and artifact.

## Do-nothing wins

A hypothetical team already completes its job in a shared spreadsheet. The subject's new integration reduces one step but imposes migration/training cost. The wedge records that counterevidence and proposes an authorized buyer task comparison. It does not call a feature difference a moat or forge customer quotes.

## Unknown arithmetic stays unknown

Eligible accounts and price are supplied but reachable fraction is null/unknown. The helper returns incomplete with no computed revenue. Setting unknown to zero would hide a missing decision; a fabricated 1% conversion would falsely complete it.

## Refresh failure is not product removal

A formerly documented feature page returns 403 on refresh. Preserve the old source/hash, append an unavailable/unknown revision and report decision impact. Never replace old supported with unsupported from a failed fetch. A stale price warns under the explicit expiry policy even if source hash matches.
