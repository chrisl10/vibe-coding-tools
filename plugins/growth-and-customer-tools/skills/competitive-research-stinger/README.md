# Competitive Research guide map

Existing guides 01-06 cover scope, research, XLSX, branded PDF, market insights and delivery QA. New guides 07-09 cover buyer alternatives/wedges, reachable-market scenarios and dated fact refresh. The extension schema and offline validator preserve provenance, labels, expiry and arithmetic; they do not validate market demand or comparative performance. Runtime research uses actual available capabilities and account/role-level inputs. See examples/competitive-outcome-truth-cases.md for provisional and false-proof cases.

Guide 10 adds bounded web research to dated evidence packets: actual tool/query receipts, source completeness, passage support, contradictions and freshness. Use references/templates/web-research-receipt.md and examples/web-research-evidence/README.md. No new searcher or grading helper is introduced.
