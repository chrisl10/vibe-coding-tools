# Synthetic support and renewal cases

These invented cases exercise a planning procedure. No customer terms, accounts, SLA guarantees or provider actions are approved. All rates below are fixture values in fictional currency units (FCU), not market or vendor prices.

## A. Founder triage is incompatible with a 15-minute continuous target

Proposed offer: human response within 15 elapsed minutes at all times. Actual roster: founder checks at 09:00 and 15:00 America/New_York Monday-Friday, with no backup. A Friday 18:00 request has no staffed responder through the weekend. Expected result: REVISE; neither an inbox timer nor changing the target to 30 minutes fixes the gap. Customer support hours and response wording remain draft. No alert enabled and no promise published.

Supplied fictional monthly inputs: 40 tickets × 18 minutes = 12 handling person-hours; documentation 3, renewal/offboarding 2, incident reserve 4, handoff/QA 1 = 22 total person-hours. Confirmed allocation 16 person-hours gives a 6-hour demand gap. At a supplied 30 FCU/person-hour, labor demand is 660 FCU. Supplied tool cost 45 FCU gives 705 FCU, excluding UNKNOWN standby cost and other operating costs; complete total remains UNKNOWN. Demand exceeding capacity is an explicit rejection, not a recommendation to sell at 705 FCU. A doubled-ticket scenario gives 34 hours, a gap of 18 hours. Monthly hours cannot prove fast-response coverage.

## B. Business-clock edge is computed separately from elapsed time

Synthetic calendar only: staffed Monday-Friday 09:00-17:00 America/New_York, no holidays on these dates. Proposed first response: two counted business hours, receipt Friday 2026-10-09 16:30. Thirty minutes count Friday; the remaining 90 minutes end Monday 2026-10-12 10:30. If Monday is a excluded holiday under the approved calendar, the deadline becomes Tuesday 10:30 instead. The holiday choice is UNKNOWN until supplied. Expected result: clock policy requires holiday evidence and actual selected-tool exercise. No platform was configured and no employee availability was verified.

## C. Renewal and cancellation do not mean deletion

Synthetic offer: term end 2026-11-01 00:00 UTC; customer cancellation request received 2026-10-31 23:30 UTC. Notice cutoff, refund rules, entitlement end, export window and retention period are UNKNOWN. Expected result: BLOCKED for policy/contract review; record the request, avoid inventing an effective date and route billing timing to Payments. No cancellation, refund, customer reply or deletion occurs. A period-end event cannot prove application access was revoked or exports completed.

## D. A staffed inbox cannot approve incident obligations

Synthetic accepted promise supplied by caller: service-incident update every 60 elapsed minutes. Named primary absent, backup availability UNKNOWN; message includes a suspected data leak. Expected result: flag coverage failure, hand the security concern to Security using minimal metadata, retain exact accepted wording and avoid guessing legal notification deadlines. Provider uptime/SLA and refund/credit remedies remain separate evidence. Draft messages are internal only.
