# Prepare support before the first paying users

Use the existing Customer Support pair for this outcome. Start with [guide 07](07-plan-support-and-renewal-obligations.md) for customer promises, actual coverage, supplied capacity/cost inputs and lifecycle obligations. Then complete the [launch worksheet](../templates/customer-launch-support-worksheet.md). Grounding: [dated digest](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/growth-and-customer-tools/skills/customer-support-tooling-stinger/references/research/customer-launch/distillation.md). This prepares an internal review packet; no tool setup or customer-facing action is implied.

## Establish the actual starting point

Ask for team size, B2B/B2C posture, primary channel, expected conversation volume, AI requirements and intended first customers/timezones. Collect existing offer/version, accepted promises, decision owner, primary/backup acceptance, support account plan/environment and observed setup. Avoid selecting a paid tool when those inputs are absent. Tool recommendations still require guide 01's comparison table. A manual shared intake can be proposed only if it meets supplied requirements; creating it still needs exact authorization.

## Make intake and ownership explicit

List the exact contact paths customers will see and the observed destinations for each request class. Include unassigned queues, wrong-team correction, duplicate/follow-up handling, attachment constraints and safe diagnostic metadata. Distinguish support identity from product identity; Auth owns verification and recovery. Confirm a ticket owner, an acknowledged escalation recipient and a backup. Do not claim that an email address, notification or linked engineering issue creates coverage.

Calendar settings and assignment state require separate evidence. Intercom documents differing team, workspace, workflow and test-environment behavior: [hours capture](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/growth-and-customer-tools/skills/customer-support-tooling-stinger/references/research/customer-launch/raw/intercom-hours.txt). It also distinguishes assignment while away from reply reassignment: [availability capture](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/growth-and-customer-tools/skills/customer-support-tooling-stinger/references/research/customer-launch/raw/intercom-availability.txt). Pylon's account-calendar mapping is different: [hours capture](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/growth-and-customer-tools/skills/customer-support-tooling-stinger/references/research/customer-launch/raw/pylon-hours.txt). Inspect the selected provider's current semantics and account rather than treating these as universal configuration instructions.

## Prepare safe escalation and outage communication

Route billing/refund/cancellation decisions to Payments, access recovery to Auth, privacy/security issues to Security and service incident execution to the incident/runbook/status owner. Resolve optional specialist availability before invocation. Preserve one accountable ticket owner and minimal metadata while those specialists accept work. Support cannot approve refunds, grant access, promise deletion or determine legal notification deadlines.

Specify who drafts and who approves service updates, the approved customer contact policy and fallback when intake or the provider itself fails. Test shared dependencies on paper before calling the fallback independent. A status page requires a responsible update owner; an empty page or unmonitored alert provides no response guarantee. Publication and external sends retain their explicit action gates.

## Check documentation and AI defaults

Review saved replies against the current product and approved promises. Replace unsupported ETAs with an internal draft pending the actual owner's estimate. The current Plain quickstart describes AI enabled by default: [capture](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/growth-and-customer-tools/skills/customer-support-tooling-stinger/references/research/customer-launch/raw/plain-quickstart.txt). Record observed automation settings, data scope, actions, owner approval and human fallback. Existing article-count rules are internal prerequisites for consideration, not empirical guarantees or sufficient launch evidence. Autonomous deflection also needs reviewed content and actual accuracy, refusal, privacy and escalation proof with its AI/Security owners. Do not silently activate, disable or change it while preparing this packet.

## Exercise and make one recommendation

Walk through new and reopened requests, absence, calendar boundaries, provider failure, burst arrivals and restricted-topic escalation. Record expected behavior separately from observations. Use the [synthetic cases](../examples/customer-launch-support-cases.md) as paper prompts; they prove no provider behavior. Runtime drills require separately authorized account scope and sends. Reuse guide 07's capacity/clock findings instead of inventing targets, prices, staffing or universal hiring thresholds.

Recommend BLOCKED for absent critical intake/owner/security-policy evidence, REVISE for a known incompatible coverage or fallback design, or READY FOR OWNER REVIEW for a sufficiently evidenced stated support scope. Keep gaps and NOT_RUN evidence visible. The owner reviews a versioned packet, and actual launch approval remains separate. Produce the worksheet, linked obligation record, peer acceptance requirements, rehearsal evidence and one recommendation.
