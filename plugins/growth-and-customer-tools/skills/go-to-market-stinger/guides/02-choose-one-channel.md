# Choose one feasible channel
1. Compare channel families with the worksheet and cite buyer evidence beside every favorable claim. Missing buyer evidence remains unknown. Prefer no selection and a precise evidence question over a fabricated recommendation.
2. Eliminate candidates whose required cash, labor, permission, assets or response capacity exceed stated constraints. Owner budget allowance is not spend authorization. Do not treat old owner benchmarks as a guaranteed channel floor or performance forecast.
3. For evidenced search intent, consider demand capture; for evidenced repeated buyer questions and capacity to teach, consider helpful content. Neither search mechanics nor helpful content guidance proves demand, ranking, cost or fast results. Google Search, Search Central.
4. For evidenced professional discovery, consider a professional channel; verify geography and the specific targeting mechanism at use time. Public targeting options do not demonstrate intent or profitable audience size. LinkedIn.
5. Assess outbound and partnerships only from buyer access and permission evidence. Record unresolved consent/subscriber/jurisdiction questions for the legal owner; do not pronounce the campaign lawful. UK distinctions alone do not resolve an international campaign. ICO.
6. Return one provisional recommendation, its decisive evidence and closest rejected alternative. State what new observation would reverse it. A confidence label is judgment with reasons, not a statistical probability.
7. Route only after selection: Alt Ads for its covered paid platforms, Social Organic for its scope, Cold Outreach for its scope, Blogging Content Strategy for editorial work, Affiliate Referral for program mechanics. If the selected implementation lacks a qualified owner, state the capability gap. Do not expand another owner's scope or silently launch a campaign.

Cross-channel workflow is this pair's design; narrower procedure boundaries are evidenced in [owner snapshots](../references/research/distillation.md). No deterministic universal channel score is claimed.
