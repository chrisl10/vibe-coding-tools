# Design a falsifiable experiment and review evidence
1. Complete the experiment portion of the worksheet. Name one buyer/offer/channel claim and the qualifying result, then distinguish the draft from an authorized executable campaign. No actual contact, publication, platform write, tracking deployment or spending occurs here.
2. Pick a finite cash/time/exposure proposal from actual constraints. Thresholds must derive from owner economics and tolerable uncertainty; if no economics are supplied, mark the threshold undecided and ask. Do not invent CPL/conversion benchmarks.
3. Specify numerator and denominator, qualification and exclusion rules, lag window, deduplication and offline recording limits. For example, sales-qualified enquiries divided by completed tracked landing sessions is a different metric from paid customers divided by ad clicks. Zero denominator produces undefined, not zero-percent performance.
4. Record consent/privacy constraints and permitted aggregate measurement, no personal-data uploads or provider instrumentation. Record the actual attribution model and blind spots. Analytics' models allocate credit differently; attributed conversions do not prove causality. Analytics, ICO.
5. Predeclare continue/stop/revise conditions, an insufficient-exposure rule and a complaint or permission stop condition. Low volume and sales lag can leave the hypothesis unresolved; report that outcome rather than inventing success or failure. A comparative/control design can help investigate incrementality, but sufficient sample and access must be assessed separately.
6. Provide the execution handoff with evidence, assumptions, assets, measurement definition, proposed ceilings, missing approvals and selected owner. Recommendation does not authorize execution. If native role unavailable, the orchestrator reads the selected skill directly and applies the same scope; do not invent a successful dispatch.
7. On later supplied results, compare against the predeclared rules, report uncertainty and update the recommendation only with evidence. Save reports only when the caller separately authorizes a report path; a read-only role returns a packet in its response by default.
