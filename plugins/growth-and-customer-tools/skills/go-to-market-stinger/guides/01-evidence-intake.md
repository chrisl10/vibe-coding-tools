# Establish the buyer and constraints
1. Read the supplied product/buyer context and fill the [worksheet](../references/decision-worksheet.md). Record artifact pointers, observation dates and what the user authorized. Reuse aggregate evidence; do not collect named prospects.
2. Separate purchaser from user and document the triggering problem. Ask a compact batch for missing decisive details: buyer, observed discovery behavior, budget, weekly hours, timeline and qualifying result. A founder preference is not evidence of buyer behavior.
3. Read available aggregate traffic/query/conversion exports only within granted access. Mark unavailable data unknown. Public platform reach is not evidence that this buyer is present or reachable.
4. Date evidence and distinguish direct observation, user assertion, platform procedure and inference. Recheck volatile mechanics/prices from official sources before advising on execution. Fetch date is not a publication date.
5. Record product/support readiness, assets, jurisdiction and permission blockers. A marketing channel does not cure an absent offer or unserviceable customer promise.

Grounding: search's demand-capture mechanism [digest](../references/research/distillation.md), raw Google; professional targeting raw LinkedIn. These sources do not supply buyer interviews, demand or operating capacity.
