# Constructed decision example: professional workflow tool
This entire buyer scenario is fictional, for procedural review. No customer interview, campaign, conversion or market finding occurred. Numbers below are constructed inputs, not benchmarks or recommended universal thresholds.

Suppose a founder supplies a permissioned aggregate buyer-journey note dated 2026-10-06: five of eight interviewed buyers discovered an alternative through useful LinkedIn practitioner posts, two through referrals and one through search. Sample came from existing customers and can overrepresent prior successful acquisition. The buyer role is an operations lead; the offer is a workflow tool whose readiness the founder asserts. Actual weekly capacity is four hours, media budget is zero and the founder already writes useful practitioner notes. No outreach or paid-spend authority was granted.

One provisional recommendation: test human organic LinkedIn content because the supplied discovery behavior and existing writing capacity fit the constraints. This inference belongs to the hypothetical evidence, not LinkedIn's demographic targeting documentation. Closest rejected alternative: paid search lacks a media budget and buyer-query evidence. Reverse the decision if a less biased discovery sample supports search instead, or organic participation cannot be sustained. Confidence remains limited by customer-sample selection and no incremental evidence.

Draft a two-week experiment: one useful practitioner note per week, at most eight total founder hours, no purchased media, only after separate publication authority. Qualifying action: an enquiry from an operations buyer with the stated workflow problem who requests a product evaluation. De-duplicate organization-level enquiries and preserve the agreed privacy boundary. Record enquiry count and completed tracked landing sessions separately; a rate with zero sessions is undefined. Anonymous exposure and offline discovery remain blind spots, and two posts do not provide controlled causal evidence.

Illustrative owner-selected rule: continue to a longer approved experiment only if at least three qualifying enquiries arrive within the agreed lag window and the founder stays within eight hours; revise if enquiry quality misses the buyer definition; record inconclusive if exposure is unknown or lag remains open. Three is an arbitrary hypothetical business decision here, not an industry target or statistical significance threshold. The real user must choose a tolerable decision rule from actual economics before execution. Stop on a permission complaint or a readiness failure. Hand off content process to Social Media Marketing Organic; the pair does not publish. No implementation or campaign has been run.
