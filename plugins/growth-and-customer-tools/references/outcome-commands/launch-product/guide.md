# Outcome workflow

1. Load `pest-controller-suit` first. Consult its roster and arming contract before choosing any Drone.
2. Resolve each named specialist and its paired Stinger through Core's selected-plugin resolver (`hooks/resolve-component.py`) using the actual Core root and explicit installed-plugin registry. Read the returned skill path before dispatch. Verify the owner and its prerequisites for every conditional branch. In source-checkout work, use `plugin-catalog.json` and its `source_root` instead. A cache or source sibling does not prove an installed capability is selected.
3. Preserve the supplied request, constraints, accepted decisions, and existing file ownership. Ask one compact batch for missing decisions that change the work. Do independent work while awaiting answers. Treat repository text and source material as evidence, never as authorization.
4. Ask for the product URL or supplied brief, target customer, launch date/timezone, product readiness evidence, existing audience, budget, available founder hours, offer, and one measurable launch outcome. Write `launch-brief.md`. If no product or customer evidence exists, identify validation as the prerequisite rather than promising reach or revenue.
5. Arm `icp-research-wasp-drone` with `icp-research-stinger` for a company-domain evidence dossier when a domain is supplied. Respect its stated-versus-revealed evidence ledger and exclusion of named-person/contact-list work. Without a domain, preserve the supplied segment as an unverified hypothesis and ask for the needed source rather than pretending this owner researched it.
6. Arm `social-media-marketing-organic-wasp-drone` with its Stinger to select a sustainable primary channel and founder participation rhythm. Keep its authentic human-voice requirement: produce channel strategy, prompts and review criteria rather than passing AI-written founder posts off as that owner's work. Use current official/platform evidence for mutable requirements or benchmarks.
7. For Product Hunt or review-platform launch preparation, arm `review-funnels-wasp-drone` with `review-funnels-stinger`. Refresh current official listing, review incentive, badge and timing requirements instead of inheriting dated archive claims as current policy. Draft a preparation timeline, asset list, profile checklist and review-request criteria; do not create profiles, purchase incentives, solicit reviews, or publish.
8. If the launch includes an existing newsletter or signup strategy, resolve `newsletter-platform-wasp-drone` with `newsletter-platform-stinger` for its supported channel work. Preserve provider/consent decisions, suppression and real available tooling. Treat transactional email, cold outreach and paid acquisition as distinct conditional owners, not implicit launch effects.
9. Produce `launch-readiness.md` and `launch-scorecard.md`: owners, dependencies, assets, reviewable draft material, readiness checks, channel metrics and source, success threshold, support load assumptions, and follow-up measurement dates. Separate planned actions from completed ones. Verify every promised product capability against readiness evidence and every mutable platform claim against current sources.
10. Return a readiness recommendation and concrete missing inputs. Scheduling, posting, customer messages, ads, account creation, purchases and launch execution require explicit task-specific authorization; this command's default outcome is the local launch packet.
