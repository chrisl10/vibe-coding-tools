# Propose admission, recovery and validation
Sources: [overload](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/platform-and-release-tools/skills/traffic-capacity-stinger/references/research/raw/google-overload.txt), [queue](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/platform-and-release-tools/skills/traffic-capacity-stinger/references/research/raw/aws-queue.txt), [retries](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/platform-and-release-tools/skills/traffic-capacity-stinger/references/research/raw/aws-retries.txt), [workload model](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/platform-and-release-tools/skills/traffic-capacity-stinger/references/research/raw/k6-arrival.txt).

1. Prepare DRAFT policy by operation/customer class: trigger signal/window/minimum evidence, bounded in-flight work and queue age/depth, priority, rejection/degradation semantics and costly rejection budget. Preserve legitimate-user fairness and paid contractual behavior. Do not promise a universal safe percentage or choose a production rate from the fictional case.
2. Trace every timeout/retry layer, maximum attempts and whole-request deadline, idempotency boundary and side effects. Propose bounded backoff/jitter where justified; stop retrying exhausted budgets or permanent errors. Identify cache expiry, synchronized reconnects and restart thundering herds. More consumers/frontends can increase DB saturation and retry load.
3. Define recovery gates from fresh same-population tail latency, error rate, saturation, queue oldest age and net drain, sustained for an owner-approved duration. Include hysteresis and gradual reopening proposal, cache/warmup/replay effects, dead-letter/manual reconciliation owner, stale-data branch and rollback decision. No-data is inconclusive, not recovered. Do not execute recovery.
4. Draft later test acceptance: actual owned target/environment/revision, authorized endpoints/dependencies, synthetic data and credential boundaries, arrival model and mix, maximum rates/concurrency/ramp/duration, total request and money budget, generator resource limits, observers, exact stop thresholds and operator, recovery observations and evidence destination. Third-party calls need their own scope. Missing owner acceptance stays DRAFT and NOT_RUN.
5. A proposed test distinguishes client attempts/admissions/completions and dropped iterations. Choose the application's existing authorized test facility with its owner; install no new tool and run no distributed benchmark. Negative branches include wrong target, production ambiguity, no cost cap, downstream harm, telemetry loss and inability to stop.
6. Return the completed worksheet, evidence ledger, sensitivity arithmetic and proposed admission/recovery/test policy. Route authorized implementation separately through DevOps/Vercel/DB/Sentry and Security; selected-owner resolution and actual host availability are required. Planning/source checks do not establish server, customer, native or performance proof.
