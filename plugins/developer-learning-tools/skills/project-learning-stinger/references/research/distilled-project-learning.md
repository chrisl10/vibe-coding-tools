## Wasp Nest teaching policy

Prediction, a short attempt, a targeted hint, retry and teach-back are authored tutor procedures. They are an explicit teaching policy supported by the scope of guided practice, not a measured intervention or a guarantee. Adaptive routing uses the actual observed task and support needed. It does not diagnose intelligence, disability, motivation or job readiness. Exercise time and pacing remain learner-specific.

## Format evidence

The six-field skill surface, reduced Cursor projection and generated Codex carrier follow Queen's current per-type guide and primary harness archive. Source validity is separate from installation, selection, role discovery and native execution.
