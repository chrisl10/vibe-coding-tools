The contract-first lesson, changed-condition question and three deliberate faults are authored pedagogy, not empirical claims about learning efficacy. Boundary cases come from the exercise's explicit contract, not a universal framework rule. Metamorphic checks require a relationship independently promised by the requirement; do not assume linearity, symmetry or monotonicity from code. Framework docs do not establish that a learner understands testing or that a product has sufficient coverage.

Sources were retrieved on 2026-10-07. They are current official pages, with unknown publication dates; no claim that all were published within six months. HTML bytes, normalized text and fetch/hash records are retained. Python's current online documentation may be newer than the local interpreter; the fixture uses only established stdlib unittest APIs and records its actual interpreter.
