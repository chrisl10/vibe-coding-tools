# Review and handoff

1. Walk every claim against its scene and matching source/runtime evidence. Check the version, environment, account permissions and tier. An absent artifact, hash mismatch, changed feature flag, unsupported state or mock removes OBSERVED eligibility until resolved.
2. Inspect the final media through the selected capture owner. Review all frames or the documented review coverage, captions, narration, thumbnails, metadata and text exports for sensitive content. Opaque selected masks help protect selected pixels; they do not prove unknown regions, audio or derived artifacts are safe. Do not upload uncertain artifacts to a provider or sharing site to inspect them.
3. Reconcile final scene timing against captions and narration. Test actual playback and caption controls on the intended player and device. Check essential visual explanation, speaker identification, meaningful sounds, control/error visibility and readable tutorial text. Record tests actually performed and blind spots; do not label a worksheet as an accessibility pass.
4. Check the successful path and material failure/limit, synthetic/mock/time-edit disclosures, and unsupported claims. No invented customers, testimonials, results, speed, privacy or security claims. Omitted setup and privileged fixtures must not be presented as an ordinary user's capability.
5. Return a release decision of DRAFT, NEEDS_EVIDENCE or READY_FOR_OWNER_REVIEW. The latter means the packet can be reviewed, not publication authorization. Keep raw originals and edit provenance under the capture owner's private-output rules; the planner does not move, delete, chmod or distribute them.
6. File the report under the consumer repository `library/requirements/reports/product-demo/` through an authorized writing orchestrator. Read-only roles return the complete report text for that orchestrator to save. Include routes/states covered, artifact locators, claim classification, cuts/disclosures, missing native/player/learner tests, environment effects and remaining approvals. Publication, provider jobs, spending and implementation remain separate explicit actions.

Use [negative cases](../references/negative-cases.json) as review scenarios. Their expected decisions are guidance, not claims of executed browser or learner tests.
