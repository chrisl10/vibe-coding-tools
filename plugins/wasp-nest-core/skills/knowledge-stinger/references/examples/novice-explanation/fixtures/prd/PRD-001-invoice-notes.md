# Invoice notes proposal
Status: Draft
Revision: 1

Only workspace owners may approve an invoice note for an invoice in their own workspace.
An owner must first review the draft note, then approve it, then explicitly request sending it.
Approval alone never sends the note. Sending requires a separate action.
Members may read notes within their permitted workspace but may not approve or send them.
Automated reminders are a future proposal and require another accepted decision before implementation.
Retention duration is undecided. Do not choose a duration from this document.
No implementation, deployment, delivery or market-demand evidence is supplied.
