# go-to-market-wasp-drone

**Plugin owner:** growth-and-customer-tools. **Required plugins:** wasp-nest-core plus selected owner. **Trigger policy:** on-demand before channel execution.
**Proposed source:** packs/growth-and-customer-tools/agents/go-to-market-wasp-drone.md paired with packs/growth-and-customer-tools/skills/go-to-market-stinger/SKILL.md. This stage is not catalog registration.

## Domain
Cross-channel acquisition selection against a specific buyer's observed discovery behavior, intent, capacity, budget and readiness. Returns one provisional recommendation and a falsifiable experiment draft, with authority limits.

## Trigger phrases
- Which acquisition channel should I start with for this buyer?
- Paid search, content, outbound or partners with these hours and budget?
- Where should I test finding customers before setting up campaigns?

## Do NOT route when
- A channel is already chosen and needs implementation: Alt Ads, Social Organic, Cold Outreach or the actual selected implementation owner.
- The task is public ICP discovery or competitive market evidence: ICP Research / Competitive Research.
- The task is broad positioning, pricing, financial forecasting or a legal conclusion: qualified business/economics/legal owner.
- Named prospect lists, messages, campaign launches or provider configuration: outside this pair's read-only scope.

## Inputs and outputs
Need buyer/problem, dated discovery evidence, constraints, geography, readiness and qualifying result. Missing decisive fields trigger a compact question rather than invented selection. Output evidence worksheet, one recommendation, rejected alternative/reversal condition, bounded experiment draft and selected implementation handoff. No market success proof.

## Sequence and directives
ICP/competitive evidence if necessary -> this pair -> selected channel owner after separate execution authority. Resolve installed selected owner before dispatch; native unavailable permits bounded direct-skill use by the orchestrator. Return packet in response; orchestrator saves only to a caller-authorized path. No hidden campaign, spending, tracking or messaging action.
