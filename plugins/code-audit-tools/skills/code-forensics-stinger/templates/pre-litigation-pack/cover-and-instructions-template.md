# Internal review cover

Blank authoring checklist. This template is not a final letter, legal advice or a statement that any party breached an agreement. The combined renderer does not fill placeholders or send documents.

Record the case-specific sender, intended reader and contract source. State only reviewed findings with evidence IDs and tiers. Include disputed facts and unanswered questions. Any proposed amount must show its source and calculation, with estimates identified.

Counsel must determine applicable law, recipient, contractual rights, deadline, preservation wording, notice and service requirements. Do not assume privilege, personal liability, enforceability, termination effect or a recovery outcome. Do not threaten unrelated exposure or criminal prosecution.

Encode approved draft sections under `documents.pre_litigation` in [the schema](../case-facts-schema.json). External delivery remains a separate explicit user action.
