# Principles

## Evidence rules

Record sources before writing findings. A captured invoice can establish what the invoice says; it does not by itself establish payment or delivery. Mark each report block PROVEN, DERIVED or HYPOTHESIS and cite the matching evidence IDs. PROVEN means the case author has checked that specific statement against the source. The renderer cannot verify that check. Mark estimates with `estimate: true`; they cannot use PROVEN.

Preserve originals and their acquisition hashes in the private case workspace. Keep counterevidence and missing records visible. Do not infer motive, wrongdoing or legal liability from low activity, a price difference or a missing record alone. Counsel determines applicable law, legal conclusions, service, deadlines and any privilege status.

## Work from evidence

Ask what the user wants reviewed. Record the scope, acquisition method, date range and access permission for each source. An allegation is a hypothesis until supported. Distinguish an invoice, a payment receipt, a contract promise and a delivery record.

## Conservative inference

A billing gap estimate is DERIVED and is not a substitute invoice. A code-volume estimate is a heuristic, not measured labor. No commits does not establish no work. No log entries does not establish no activity. Preserve reasonable alternative explanations.

## Documents are drafts

Human review determines whether authored text is fair, accurate and suitable for its audience. Counsel decides any legal framing. Do not assert privilege through a label, or recommend litigation tactics from a previous matter. No files are sent externally by these helpers.
