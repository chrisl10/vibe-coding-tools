# Source quality tiers and the fact-check standard

## Tiers

| Tier | What | Role |
|---|---|---|
| A | Primary documents: court filings, bills and votes, SEC filings, official statements, transcripts, the actual repo or paper, on-record video | Best possible receipt. One Tier A source can carry a post alone if it directly shows the claim. |
| B | Established news organizations and major trade press with named reporters and correction policies (wires, national outlets across the spectrum, major tech press) | Standard receipt. Two independent Tier B sources confirming the same core fact clear the gate. |
| C | Verified, named accounts on X posting first-hand claims; specialist newsletters; company blogs | Usable as the story lead or color, but the core claim still needs Tier A or two Tier B confirmations. |
| D | Anonymous accounts, aggregator screenshots, "BREAKING" accounts with no sourcing, AI-generated news sites | Never a receipt. Discovery only. If the story exists only at Tier D, it does not get posted. |

## The fact-check standard (applies to the ONE chosen topic only)

1. Identify the single core factual claim the post will ride on. Write it as one sentence.
2. Confirm it with Tier A, or two independent Tier B sources. Independent means separate reporting, not two outlets citing the same tweet.
3. Confirm the timestamps: the development itself must be inside the last 24 hours. A fresh article about an old event does not reset the clock; the EVENT must be new.
4. Check for a correction, retraction, or credible denial before writing. Search the claim plus "denies", "false", "correction".
5. Numbers in the post must match the receipts exactly. No rounding a number up to make it hit harder.
6. If confirmation fails at any step, the topic is dead. State that it failed verification and end the run. Do not fall back to a second topic with a second research pass; the one-topic rule exists to protect token spend.

## Accuracy boundaries for this skill

- Sharp framing of true facts is allowed. Fabrication, fabricated quotes, invented numbers, and misleading edits are never the point. Every post must survive its own receipts being opened.
- Beats, topics to avoid or emphasize, and the editorial policy are the user's to set in `references/editorial-preferences.md`. The default policy is politically neutral (no partisan advocacy, contested issues presented fairly). Preferences change which candidates are picked; they never relax the fact-check standard above.
