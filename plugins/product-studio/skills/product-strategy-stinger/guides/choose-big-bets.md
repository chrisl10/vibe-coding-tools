# Choose the big bets

Fills section 5. Questions: Batch 4.

A bet is a choice about where to put scarce time and money, with a reason and a way to be proven wrong. Strategy forces that choice [raw/lenny-strategy-blocks.html]. Calling them bets helps people admit they might be wrong; Amplitude found executives accept "bet" language because it carries risk and return [raw/amplitude-north-star-checklist.html].

## Steps

1. **Gather candidates** from the problem, the job, and positioning. Good candidates sound like "Win solo freelancers before agencies" or "Make setup take under five minutes", not "Build an invoices page".
2. **Score them lightly.** For each candidate ask: how big is the impact, how sure are we, do we know what levers to pull, and are those levers something only we can pull? These four come from Strategy Blocks; team judgment is fine when data is missing [raw/lenny-strategy-blocks.html].
3. **Have the owner pick 2 to 4.** This is the house rule for a v1 strategy. Strategy Blocks uses 3 to 5 pillars for a company or team [raw/lenny-strategy-blocks.html]; a new product needs fewer.
4. **For each bet, write** why the owner believes it, what would prove it wrong, and which input metric it should move (fill the input column after [set the north star](set-north-star.md)).
5. **Write what we are NOT betting on.** At least one. A strategy names its non-focus areas [raw/lenny-strategy-blocks.html].
6. **Turn each "why we believe it" that has weak evidence into an assumption** in section 8. Name load-bearing assumptions out loud, with a warning signal, so you notice when one breaks [raw/mtp-three-products-three-failures.html].

## Watch for

- Feature lists dressed as bets. Ask "what choice does this make?"
- More than 4 bets. Ask which one the owner would drop if time were cut in half.
- Bets that only make sense if a date is promised. SVPG warns that the push for predictability crowds out outcomes [raw/svpg-predictability-vs-outcomes.html].

## Done when

2 to 4 bets, each with a disproof signal, plus a "not betting on" line. Log the choice as a decision note.
