# Position the product

Fills section 4. Questions: Batch 3. Method: April Dunford's five parts, in her order [raw/dunford-positioning-quickstart.html].

## Steps

1. **Competitive alternatives.** Ask what customers would do if this product did not exist. Always include "do nothing" or "keep doing it by hand". Drop "phantom competitors" the owner has never actually lost to [raw/dunford-positioning-quickstart.html].
2. **Unique attributes.** What does this product have that those alternatives do not? Features, approach, data, know-how.
3. **Value.** For each attribute, ask "so what?" until you reach something the customer cares about (time saved, money, less risk).
4. **Best-fit customers.** Who cares the most about that value? Go back and sharpen section 2 if this changes it.
5. **Market category.** Pick the frame that makes the value obvious. Recommend an existing category or a niche of one. Creating a new category is slower and costlier, and most companies start in an existing one [raw/dunford-positioning-quickstart.html].
6. **Write the one-sentence summary** at the top of the strategy from these parts.
7. **Set a check-in date.** Dunford recommends a structured positioning check about every six months, on competitors, differences, and value [raw/mtp-dunford-position-for-future.html]. Put the date in the doc and log it as a decision.

## Do not

- Write a fill-in-the-blanks positioning statement first. Dunford calls that exercise potentially dangerous because it assumes one answer per blank [raw/dunford-positioning-quickstart.html]. The summary sentence comes last, from the five parts.
- Confuse positioning with a tagline or brand story.
- Claim the product is better without saying better than what, for whom.

## When to hand off

If the owner names many alternatives and cannot rank them, or wants evidence about competitors, offer `competitive-research-stinger` and link its report. A good solution has to beat the alternatives by enough that customers switch [raw/svpg-build-to-learn-vs-build-to-earn.html], so knowing the real alternatives matters.

## Done when

All five rows are filled or marked OPEN, and the one-sentence summary uses only those answers.
