# Find and plan the riskiest assumption test

Fills section 8. Questions: Batch 7.

## Steps

1. **Collect assumptions** from every earlier section: "how we know" answers that were beliefs, bet reasons, the north-star link to revenue, v1 feasibility, and who pays.
2. **Phrase each as something that must be true,** and make it specific. "Freelancers will connect their bank account in the first session" beats "users like it" [raw/producttalk-assumption-testing.html].
3. **Tag the type:** desirability, viability, feasibility, usability, or ethical [raw/producttalk-assumption-testing.html]. Do not argue over the label; the list exists to surface risk. Give viability extra attention: SVPG says it was long underrated, and for AI products it covers cost, pricing, liability, and ethics [raw/svpg-strong-opinions-loosely-held.html].
4. **Rate importance and evidence.** The riskiest assumptions are the ones that matter most and have the least proof (assumption mapping) [raw/producttalk-assumption-testing.html].
5. **Pick one riskiest assumption** with the owner. Recommend the high-importance, no-evidence one.
6. **Plan the smallest test.** Build only enough to test that one unknown, not a whole product (the Riskiest Assumption Test idea) [raw/hackernoon-riskiest-assumption-test.html]. Test one assumption, not the whole idea [raw/producttalk-assumption-testing.html].
7. **Set pass and fail before the test.** Write the threshold and what you will change if it fails.
8. **Add an early warning signal** for each load-bearing assumption so you notice if it breaks later [raw/mtp-three-products-three-failures.html].

## Common traps

- "People loved the demo" is not "people will pay". Treat those as two separate assumptions [raw/mtp-three-products-three-failures.html].
- A competitor existing proves someone buys, but not which buyer or channel works for you [raw/mtp-useful-isnt-enough.html].

## When to hand off

If the test needs interview scripts, an opportunity tree, or experiment design, hand off to `discovery-research-stinger` and link its plan. This skill names the assumption and the pass line; discovery owns the method.

## Done when

An assumption table, one riskiest assumption, a smallest test with pass and fail lines, an owner, and a date.
