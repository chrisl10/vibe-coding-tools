# Evidence driven document catalogue

Select documents by their reader, purpose and source coverage. The full packet is extensive when the library supports it. Do not produce eighteen nearly identical documents or invent sections to reach this menu's size.

| Document | Reader and decision | Minimum useful source material |
|---|---|---|
| Product explained | New reader understands the product and its value | Supported purpose, core features, actors and at least one grounded journey |
| Product atlas | Team understands the complete feature set | Feature/family census, subfeatures, terminology and action boundaries |
| Founder brief | Leadership chooses direction and risk | Buyer hypotheses, user types, journeys, benefits, risks and business decisions |
| Investor brief | Investor judges the thesis and proof | Supported buyer/product/economic account; explicit missing metrics rather than invented traction |
| Public brief | Buyer/partner understands approved public value | Public-safe feature facts, use cases and approved source labels |
| Competitive analysis | Leadership/sales chooses position and product gaps | Official peer features/pricing/rights, subject evidence, dated findings and actionable edge decisions |
| Research foundations | Reader understands why a design choice is reasonable | Primary publications with methods, dates, limitations and source-to-product mapping |
| Decision and authority map | Teams delegate within the rules | Actor/role/permission contracts, standing rules and reserved decisions |
| Golden business journeys | Teams share a business-outcome model | Real records, actors, handoffs, exception paths and closure evidence |
| Claims and proof handbook | Commercial team makes defensible claims | Claim/source map, actual boundaries, approved language and unmeasured-result register |
| Engineering behavior atlas | Engineer changes behavior correctly | Current contracts, code where available, failure cases and acceptance evidence |
| Dependency and change map | Engineer/product lead scopes a change | Cross-feature interfaces, consumers, dependencies and source revisions |
| Failure and recovery field guide | Operations diagnoses interrupted work | State model, receipts, uncertainty, retry/resume rules and escalation ownership |
| Launch readiness register | Teams commit to supported capability | Product promise, current configuration/bindings and qualification evidence |
| Buyer fit and discovery | Sales qualifies a real problem | Source-backed target users/problems; clearly labelled hypotheses and poor-fit criteria |
| Demo storybook and battlecards | Sales demonstrates the whole result | Supported journey, comparable rival facts and actual demo/proof conditions |
| Service diagnostic and escalation guide | Service routes an incident correctly | Symptoms, states, channel rights, evidence and responsible teams |
| Account activation and first value | Account manager establishes useful adoption | Prerequisites, initial workflow, ownership and accepted-result milestones |
| Account health and value review | Account manager evaluates adoption/expansion | Defined signals, customer baselines if supplied and outcome/cost records |
| Founder decision book | Founder chooses what to build | Source-linked gaps, alternatives, recommendation, consequence and acceptance cases |
| Economics and credits explainer | Commercial/support team explains charges | Accepted units, rates with dates, fee/usage model and clearly labelled worked examples |
| Team onboarding reader | New team member learns shared meanings | Existing supported documents, vocabulary and role-specific reading paths |
| Engineering handoff | Coordinator triages findings | Full findings register, gap types, proposed priorities, affected contracts and checks |

These are owner-derived document choices, not research facts about market demand. A customer example is factual only when supplied and approved. Illustrative examples must be labelled and must not invent results, prices, customers or deployed capability. Audience-first writing is supported by DigitalGov; coverage and no-quota rules are W02-W05 in [the distillation](research/distilled-library-packets.md).
