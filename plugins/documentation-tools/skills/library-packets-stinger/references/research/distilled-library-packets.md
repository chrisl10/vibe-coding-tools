## Thin evidence and implementation cautions

The citation archive explains how locators work; it supplies no independent validation of a library's factual claims. Citations are provenance aids, not a truth certificate. The grade-8 target is the owner's requirement. The DigitalGov index supports writing for the audience and testing understanding, but gives no numeric reading formula or pass threshold. [Citations](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/documentation-tools/skills/library-packets-stinger/references/research/raw/anthropic-citations.md), [DigitalGov](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/documentation-tools/skills/library-packets-stinger/references/research/raw/digitalgov-plain-language.md), [forge topic](../../reports/forge-topic.md)

The supplementary quickstart now supplies documented picture insertion, and the LibreOffice archive supplies a documented headless conversion path. Their availability in a particular harness/runtime and the success of a particular conversion still require local qualification. The ReportLab archive covers PDF layout, while pypdf documents extraction limitations; neither substitutes for rendering and visual inspection. Choose available tools in the permitted environment and report unavailable output/review capabilities honestly. These sources do not authorize installation or establish identical Word/PDF pagination. [Word quickstart](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/documentation-tools/skills/library-packets-stinger/references/research/raw/python-docx-quickstart.md), [LibreOffice CLI](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/documentation-tools/skills/library-packets-stinger/references/research/raw/libreoffice-start-parameters.md), [ReportLab](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/documentation-tools/skills/library-packets-stinger/references/research/raw/reportlab-platypus.md), [pypdf](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/documentation-tools/skills/library-packets-stinger/references/research/raw/pypdf-text-extraction.md), [forge topic](../../reports/forge-topic.md)
