# What the invoice note proposal asks for

This Draft PRD describes an invoice-note approval and sending process. It supplies no evidence that the process has been built or used. [P1]

Only an owner may approve a note for an invoice in that owner's workspace. The owner reviews the draft first, approves it second and requests sending third. Approval by itself never sends a note. A separate sending action is required. [P2]

Members may read notes within their permitted workspace. They may not approve or send them. Being a member does not grant owner powers. [P3]

Automated reminders are proposed future work. Another accepted decision is required before implementing them. The retention duration is still undecided, so this PRD does not tell us how long to keep a note. [P4]

An owner reviewing a draft is like a supervisor checking a letter before authorizing someone to mail it. Approval and mailing are separate steps. This analogy does not imply postal delivery, automatic sending, successful software execution or permission to use another workspace. [P5, illustration]

A PRD is a document describing desired product behavior. Draft means the proposal has not been presented here as accepted authority. Retention duration means how long the information is kept; its value remains open in this source. [P1, P4]

Check your understanding: If an owner approves a note, what must happen before it is sent, and may an ordinary member perform those actions?
