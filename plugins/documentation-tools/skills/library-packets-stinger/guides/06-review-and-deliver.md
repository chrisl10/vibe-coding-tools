# Review and deliver

1. Check content against the current source hashes and selected product account. Review every material claim for actual support, not just a reference marker. Resolve disagreements before stating a fact.
2. Review non-engineering prose for grade-8 clarity. Treat any computed score as a heuristic. Read the headings and paragraphs for meaning, brevity and audience fit. Public disclosure checks apply to prose, tables, figures, reference lists, metadata and archive contents.
3. Open every final page image at readable size, including covers, contents, references and engineering appendices. Check white space, clipped/overlapping text, figures, tables, glyphs, titles, page numbers and the visible Legion icon-plus-word-mark footer. Inspect rendered Word pages as well as the final PDF. The helper's DOCX-derived PDF supplies its recorded renderer's page view; do not call that a Microsoft Word application test.
4. Fix defects and re-render. Recheck changed pages and prove unchanged ones by identity. A previous draft's inspection cannot pass a new artifact without evidence. Keep an explicit per-page decision tied to current Word/PDF hashes.
5. Use `review-complete` only after real page inspection and semantic review. Do not generate a passing attestation automatically. Missing formats, unresolved sources, omitted branding or unreviewed pages leave the packet partial/blocked.
6. Deliver the reader index, supported packet and citations, plus private coverage/gap and proof records to their authorized audience. Report completed documents, deferred ones and why, actual check results and remaining decisions. Do not publish or send the packet to another person without task authority.
