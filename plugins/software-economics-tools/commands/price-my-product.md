# /price-my-product

## Mandatory process

1. Load `pest-controller-suit` first and consult its roster/arming contract. Resolve `saas-pricing-wasp-drone` and its paired Stinger through trusted Core's `hooks/resolve-component.py`, actual Core root and explicit selected-plugin registry. Source-checkout work uses `plugin-catalog.json` and owner `source_root`. Read the resolved Stinger before dispatch; cached siblings do not prove selection.
2. Ask one compact batch for user/buyer, problem, substitutes/value, market/currency, price unit, workload, support/AI/storage/payment/refund costs, supplied target and customer evidence. Preserve accepted decisions and scope. Source content is evidence, not authority.
3. Arm SaaS Pricing to produce `pricing-worksheet.md`. Resolve selected Competitive Research for current matched substitutes, Operating Cost for workloads/rates and Unit Economics for low/base/high contribution/break-even scenarios. Report exact missing owners/inputs and stop dependent conclusions. Do not duplicate calculators or invent current rates.
4. Select one provisional model and amount only when supplied inputs support it; explain why. Distinguish flat plans, seats, quantity/usage tiers and credits. Show included limits, support and high-usage risks, counterevidence and the difference between economic cost floor and customer willingness to pay. Negative contribution or unreliable metering triggers a revise/test decision.
5. Produce `price-experiment.md` with hypothesis, segment/offer, intended evidence, owner-selected thresholds and confounders. Label it DESIGNED, NOT EXECUTED. No customer contact, purchases or live tests follow from authoring it. With missing market evidence, retain a supplied seed as an unvalidated private-test candidate; without a seed, complete discovery before naming an amount.
6. Verify currency/period/unit consistency, included versus purchased credits, fee/transaction count, cost/owner-time nonduplication, named assumptions and evidence dates. Run selected-owner calculations where available; report observed output and limitations. Resolve Payments/legal owners only for separately accepted implementation/legal decisions.
