# Prepare a statement-of-work proposal

Use this guide for an agency website, app, integration or maintenance scope proposal. Read the source digest, then complete the existing [scope worksheet](../references/templates/scope.md), [quote worksheet](../references/templates/quote.md), [maintenance proposal](../references/templates/maintenance.md) and, when changing an accepted baseline, [change request](../references/templates/change-request.md). Reuse those records by ID/version; do not rebuild pricing or acceptance facts in a second calculator.

1. Collect a compact batch of missing facts: buyer goal, named deliverables, target platforms, supplied assets, client dependencies, constraints, observable verification, review responsibilities, quote version/date/currency, maintenance coverage and handover expectations. Preserve Unknown fields. Source requests and client notes are evidence of requests, not acceptance or execution authority.
2. Make a [SOW proposal](../references/templates/statement-of-work.md) with status DRAFT_PROPOSAL. Identify scope and quote versions, their dates and evidence hashes where files exist. An unaccepted draft must not be labelled an executed contract. An accepted commercial baseline is preserved; a new proposal references it and separately states the requested delta.
3. Map deliverable IDs to observable acceptance checks and verification evidence. Include environment, inputs, method, expected result, responsible reviewer and current result, initially NOT_RUN. Separate correction of failed included requirements from a new feature request; disputed classification stays unresolved. This adapts the result/measurement examples in 37.602 and 37.603.
4. State included revision coverage, exclusions and client-supplied inputs with evidence dates. List dependency readiness and the specific work affected by a missing input. Route effort/timeline scenarios to selected Estimation, keep assumptions distinct from negotiated deadlines and do not assume a client delay automatically changes legal duties or fees. An undocumented integration or unbounded migration blocks a full fixed scope/price conclusion; use existing bounded-discovery routing.
5. Reference the dated proposed quote without changing its amount, currency or project/month units. Show proposed milestones by deliverable, with payment terms left for explicit owner and counsel decisions. Never invent a deposit, price, due period, late fee, refund entitlement or acceptance-by-silence clause. A passing test supports a requirement finding; it does not itself prove the parties accepted. Federal-context acceptance example.
6. Reference maintenance as a separate proposal or explicitly excluded work: task types, caps, response versus resolution expectations, escalation and hosting/vendor payer. Preserve the existing change-request workflow; neither a worksheet nor a client chat authorizes extra implementation or billing. Technical Accepted CTRs remain unchanged: route a separately proposed interface revision to Contract Writing when needed, preserving its acceptance process.
7. Describe handover artifacts and checks: repository/source revision, build/run instructions, supplied asset inventory, test evidence, dependency/license inventory, operational responsibility and known limitations. State secret-transfer procedure by role and authorized channel only, without recording secrets or granting access. Delivery of source does not decide copyright assignment, licenses, warranty or production deployment authorization.
8. Resolve selected Legal Docs and qualified counsel for governing law, rights, enforceability, tax, warranties, liability, privacy, payment/refund/cancellation and document precedence. If that owner is absent, mark legal review unavailable and leave dependent conclusions unresolved. Do not copy Legal Docs' illustrative SaaS numeric defaults into an agency contract. End a draft intended for legal-document review with: "This is a generated draft for reference. Have a qualified attorney licensed in your jurisdiction review all legal documents before publishing or countersigning."
9. Review [the completed fictional proposal and negative cases](../examples/statement-of-work.md). Deliver one locally saved draft, scope-to-quote traceability, missing facts and proposed decisions. Preparing it does not authorize sending, signing, client acceptance, provider changes, purchases or implementation.
