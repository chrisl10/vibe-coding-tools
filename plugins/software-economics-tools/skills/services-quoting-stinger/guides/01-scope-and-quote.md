# Scope a service quotation

Read the [distillation](../references/research/distillation.md), [model](../references/model.md) and [scope worksheet](../references/templates/scope.md). Ask one compact batch for deliverables, acceptance checks, platforms/integrations, supplied assets, ownership, revisions, migration, deployment responsibility, target date, budget and maintenance expectations. Explain unfamiliar terms simply.

1. Record deliverable IDs with observable acceptance checks, included revisions and exclusions. A website quote needs page count, content owner, forms/CMS/accessibility targets and launch handoff. An app quote needs roles, data, integrations, migration, recovery and verification boundaries.
2. Resolve Estimation through trusted Core's selected-owner resolver and read its paired Stinger. Use its effort evidence including learning, verification and rework; do not implement another forecast or divide story points into dates. No universal AI speed multiplier. If unavailable, collect supplied effort scenarios and label forecasting incomplete.
3. Freeze a reviewable scope version. Unavailable API documentation/access, unknown data quality or unbounded integration behavior blocks a full fixed quote. Recommend a separately scoped discovery deliverable with explicit price cap and acceptance; do not treat this recommendation as buyer acceptance or purchase authorization. FAR provides a federal risk example, not private law. [Archive](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/services-quoting-stinger/references/research/raw/far-application.md).
4. Collect dated cost/rate inputs, defining whether owner hourly input is loaded cost or opportunity valuation. It is counted exactly once. Separate contractor/cash costs, project allocations, pass-through expenses and recurring costs. SBA supports one-time/monthly separation. [Archive](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/services-quoting-stinger/references/research/raw/sba-plan.md).
5. Build supplied lower/base/upper scenarios where evidence supports them. Require a reason for effort, revision or risk differences. Choose one pricing method: markup or target margin. Contingency is an explicit uncertainty allowance and does not replace planned surplus. Follow [model](../references/model.md) and the [examples](../examples/quotes.md). Never manufacture prevailing market rates.
6. Produce [quote worksheet](../references/templates/quote.md) with one recommended proposed price/model and its basis, sensitivity, timeline assumptions, payment milestone proposal, validity period requiring owner selection and unresolved exclusions. It remains a proposal until owner and counterparty accept through their actual process.
7. Resolve Unit Economics for business-wide contribution and break-even, Legal Docs for jurisdiction/privacy/service-contract concerns, and Contract Writing only for technical interface agreements. Do not claim the technical contract specialist supplies legal negotiation advice. Missing selected owners block those dependent conclusions.
8. Verify scope-to-price traceability, distinct project/month units, no double-counted owner cost, review effort, reserve versus margin and optional calculator output. Do not send, sign, charge or configure billing.
