# Fictional worked quotations

All rates below are supplied hypothetical USD assumptions dated 2026-10-07. They are not market benchmarks. Owner rate is a cost/valuation, not the hourly price charged to a buyer. Effort already includes learning, review and rework supplied by Estimation.

| Case and scope | Cost calculation | Pricing calculation | Proposed result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website: five pages, supplied text/assets, one revision round, observable form handoff | 40h × 50 + 200 fixed = 2200; 10% reserve = 220; C=2420 | 25% markup: 2420 × 1.25 | USD 3025/project; planned surplus 605, margin 20% |
| App: bounded login, one role and documented integration, migrations excluded | 120h × 60 + 1000 contractor + 200 fixed = 8400; 15% reserve =1260; C=9660 | 25% target margin: 9660 ÷ .75 | USD 12880/project; surplus 3220; separate USD 100 pass-through makes total 12980 |
| Maintenance: capped four owner hours monthly, known tasks/support window | 4h × 50 + 40 monthly allocation =240; 10% reserve =24; C=264 | 25% markup: 264 ×1.25 | USD 330/month; surplus 66; no unlimited support |

Sensitivity: six maintenance hours makes C=(300+40)×1.10=374 and service price 467.50/month at the same markup. Website revision change: additional 8h×50, no repeated fixed expense, 10% reserve, 25% markup gives 550/project delta; proposed total 3575. It does not alter the accepted baseline automatically.

Unknown integration: buyer wants synchronization with an undocumented vendor API and historical data migration. Full fixed quote stops because effort is unbounded. Recommend one discovery deliverable: prove authentication, inspect a provided data sample and test one reversible read-only workflow, with an owner-chosen cap and acceptance. Credentials and provider operations still require explicit authorization. No calculator can turn a missing input into a defensible number.

These formulas follow [model](../references/model.md); one-time/month separation is grounded in SBA. Unknown integration stop is our advisory policy informed by FAR's bounded-specification example, without claiming it governs this private quote. Business-level break-even stays with Unit Economics, and timing stays with Estimation.
