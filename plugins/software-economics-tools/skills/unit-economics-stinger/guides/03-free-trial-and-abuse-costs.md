# Model free users, trial service and abuse costs

Use the [cohort worksheet](../references/templates/free-trial-worksheet.md) and dated distillation. This extends the advisory worksheet while reusing the existing calculator. It creates no fraud control or live billing action.

1. Establish period, currency, price supplied by the user, observed cohort volumes, billed attempts, settled receipts, refunds, service intervals, and cost sources. Funnel stages overlap: a lead can trial and pay in one period. Count service intervals and unique cost IDs instead of adding all stage counts as unique people. Unknown volumes, attribution or rates remain questions, never zero.
2. Separate legitimate free service, trial service, signup failure, suspected abusive service and blocked attempts. For every expensive action locate the charge boundary: rejected before upstream dispatch, upstream attempted, completed, retried, or retained stored output. Blocking later cannot undo already-spent work. Infrastructure and fee consequences are grounded in card-testing documentation; attribution for this application still needs supplied evidence.
3. Build quantity × supplied rate totals for each cohort, including signup email/SMS, storage, egress, AI requests, support and failed authorization where observed. Include acquisition spending once for the chosen period, without claiming CAC payback or lifetime value. Partition paid service to avoid charging refunded/failed units twice. The calculator applies its variable service cost to every attempted paid billing unit. If successful, failed and refunded service have different costs, enter the actual aggregate paid-service total as a held period cost and remove the uniform variable row. State that all service volume is then held constant in the break-even calculation.
4. Reconcile shared provider floors and eligible usage to supplied invoice/contract. Allocate a shared floor once, not once per cohort. Apply only known eligible credits, once in the net cost row: net eligible charge = max(0, gross eligible charge − allocated applicable credit). Excess credit is neither negative cost nor revenue. Preserve gross amount, eligibility, expiration and remaining balance outside the calculator. Organization/period compute-credit scope is evidenced in Supabase compute; actual operator terms must be refreshed.
5. Map attempted paid account-periods to `units`, supplied collected price to `price_per_unit`, unsuccessful paid attempts to `failed_payment_rate`, and fully refunded successful transactions to `refund_rate`. Free/trial starts never enter paid attempted units merely to inflate the denominator. Stripe trial and subscription lifecycle documents support that collection distinction. Partial refunds, late collections, retries as separate paid transactions, multiple prices and paid trial pricing require a separate explicit worksheet beyond this single-price model.
6. Enter held-period nonpaid cohort totals as `behavior: fixed`, with names such as `Held legitimate free service`. This is an adapter for one scenario, not a claim that free-user costs are economically fixed. Preserve their quantity/rate and scaling rule in the worksheet. Choose accounting classification from supplied policy. The output contribution excludes these held totals even if their underlying cost varies with nonpaid volume; call it paid-attempt contribution. Use `modeled_operating_surplus` for the combined period result. Break-even is conditional on the held cohort sizes, mix, costs and credits. No all-cohort margin or scalable growth threshold is inferred.
7. Compare baseline, legitimate-free growth, abuse/retry spike, high usage, no paying customers, and exhausted-credit cases. Model a proposed mitigation's overhead and incorrect blocking separately, without implementing a restriction or inferring prevented bills from request counts. Use observed quantities and documented charge boundaries, or label every sensitivity assumption fictional.
8. Run the [offline calculator](../scripts/README.md), retain input/output, reconcile costs and receipt totals, and report evidence gaps. With no paid units, do not divide nonpaid totals by zero or fabricate an infinite CAC value. No paid revenue means calculator margin ratios are null; fixed held costs show a loss. Resolve Payments for billing behavior and Security for an approved mitigation only when a separate implementation task is authorized and those owners are selected.

[Worked fictional adapter scenarios](../examples/free-trial-scenarios.md) show arithmetic and limits. They establish source behavior only, never provider rates, conversion forecasts or functioning production defenses.
