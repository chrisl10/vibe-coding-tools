# Prepare an entitlement usage cost and abuse-limit worksheet

For O281, complete [the integrated worksheet](../references/templates/fair-service-limits-worksheet.md) with the selected SaaS Pricing owner. Read the dated digest and [fictional completed cases](../examples/fair-service-limits-cases.md). This is an advisory worksheet. Rates, thresholds, customer acceptance and actual fairness require their own evidence.

1. Pin product/plan version, entitlement ID and source, environment, subject (tenant/account/user), currency/period and current policy authority. Keep feature access and usage balance distinct (F1). Preserve accepted policies verbatim; a proposed amendment is separate.
2. Define counted action or resource, unit, numerator, inclusions/exclusions, clock/timezone, rolling or fixed window, boundary/reset behavior, sum/count/last/other aggregation and sharing across tenant/seats/devices. Record retries, duplicate identifiers, failed/blocked requests, late events, freshness and reconciliation. F2-F4 explain why a billing meter, an access projection and a rate counter may have different semantics; their equality must be evidenced on the target application.
3. Attach a cost ledger with unique cost IDs, quantity, supplied dated rate/contract, charge boundary, fixed/variable behavior and supplied accounting classification. Reuse [cohort costs](03-free-trial-and-abuse-costs.md) and [the existing model](../references/model.md). Allocate shared floors and eligible credits once. Keep gross, credit eligibility/expiry/allocation and net totals visible. Unknown cost is Unknown, never zero. No duplicate calculator.
4. Model baseline, legitimate high use, suspected abuse/retries and incorrect blocking separately. A costly customer is not automatically abusive. Record classification source/confidence, unique attempts and affected subjects, served work, already-spent costs, lost value and support/recovery burden. Do not convert a suspected label into a fraud verdict.
5. Ask selected SaaS Pricing for one provisional included allowance/limit and overflow treatment grounded in buyer value, predictability and supplied economics. State threshold/unit/window, warning, overflow charge/queue/degrade/block behavior, recovery/reset, appeal/human review and review trigger. None is accepted because the arithmetic improves. With no owner threshold or value evidence, leave the number Unknown and recommend completing the missing decision fields.
6. Record the owner's chosen fairness/acceptance criteria, rationale, source/date/authority and required observation population/period. Include legitimate high-use completion, incorrect-blocking denominator, shared-account effects, warning comprehension and recovery/review evidence where the owner selects them. Record observed values and limitations separately. Missing criteria or evidence means fairness Unknown; do not supply universal thresholds. A provisional proposal cannot be marked accepted without the exact evidenced owner decision.
7. Prepare a handoff only: actual selected meter/application owner, state/counter contract, tenant binding, concurrency and retry cases, clock/reset, stale/offline behavior, reconciliation, notice and recovery acceptance cases. Resolve Payments for separately accepted server entitlement/billing implementation. Resolve the actual Security or platform owner for separately authorized abuse-control implementation. Unknown availability stays a handoff gap, with dependent implementation NOT_RUN.
8. Deliver one joined worksheet, existing calculator input/output if complete inputs fit, cost reconciliation, proposal/decision status, counterevidence, source dates, gaps and proof boundary. Actual billing, legitimate-user behavior, fairness/customer acceptance, abuse controls and installed/native execution are NOT_RUN unless separate observed evidence exists.
