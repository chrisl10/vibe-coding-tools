# Measure whether a price comparison improves the business

Use for O045 with supplied observations, or prepare an unexecuted measurement packet when data is missing. This existing SaaS Pricing owner interprets commercial evidence. Resolve selected PostHog for experiment instrumentation, Unit Economics for its supported arithmetic, Payments for accepted billing implementation and legal/accounting owners for contract or recognition assurance. Read their paired skills before handoff. No flag creation, price update, invoice/refund operation or customer send follows from this guide.

1. Complete [the worksheet](../references/templates/price-measurement-worksheet.md). Pin source extract hashes/revisions, as-of timestamps, account/environment, tenant/customer join definition, permission and de-identified fields. Preserve missing inputs. One currency and consistent service/observation period are required; stop dependent totals when FX, partial collection or undocumented costs cannot be reconciled.
2. Define eligible cohort before treatment, buyer/assignment unit, assignment period and exposure event. For account purchases, person-level price assignment can contaminate account comparisons. Preserve assigned and exposed counts separately, treatment actually delivered, variant changes, exclusions and reasons. Keep an assigned-cohort business comparison; separately label any exposed-only analysis and its selection risk. PostHog's documented metrics use exposure, so reconcile rather than silently equating them. [Digest](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/price-measurement/distillation.md), [creation](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/price-measurement/raw/posthog-creating.md), [exposure](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/price-measurement/raw/posthog-exposures.md).
3. Freeze the exact offer, price unit, limits, discount, trial, contract/grandfathering, tax convention, effective date, billing cadence and proration behavior. Preserve changes as separate revisions. Confirm actual API/SDK and legacy-trial/trial-offer mechanism with Payments; documentation capture alone proves no compatibility. A price label is not a collection, credit or refund. [Prorations](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/price-measurement/raw/stripe-prorations.md), [price update](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/price-measurement/raw/stripe-change-price.md), [trials](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/price-measurement/raw/stripe-trials.md).
4. Reconcile invoice/payment/credit/refund/dispute records to stable account and transaction IDs without duplicate capture. Keep trial-only accounts, failed collections, canceled accounts and zero spend in the relevant denominator. Define a common follow-up window per assignment or exposure and mark immature rows. State extraction delay, pending refunds/disputes, late collections and the latest reconciliation timestamp. A short window is provisional retained revenue, never lifetime value. [Metrics](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/price-measurement/raw/posthog-metrics.md), [refunds](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/price-measurement/raw/stripe-refunds.md), [disputes](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/price-measurement/raw/stripe-disputes.md).
5. Report gross collected cash, successful cash refunds, known dispute debits/recoveries, net retained cash, variable fees/delivery/support and contribution. Define taxes, FX, credits and unresolved exposures explicitly. This convention is management cash analysis, not accounting revenue recognition. Unit Economics' existing calculator supports constant-price attempted units, failed payments and full refunds under its field contract; do not force partial refunds, late collections, dispute ledgers or exposure denominators into it. Use a supplied reconciled worksheet for unsupported cases and identify the gap. [Worked arithmetic](../examples/price-measurement-cases.md).
6. Compare totals and per-assigned-unit/per-exposed-unit values with explicit denominators; zero denominators produce unavailable ratios, not zero performance. Match maturity and costs across arms. Report conversions, collections, refunds, retention and support burden as guardrails, with thresholds chosen before review by the owner. PostHog mean-of-per-user totals is not a raw business sum. Retain raw totals when clamping or filtering is used. [Metrics](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/price-measurement/raw/posthog-metrics.md).
7. Separate descriptive differences from inference. Record randomization/assignment provenance, imbalance, repeated looks, missing events, multi-variant exposure, interference, sales/marketing seasonality and concurrent product changes. Observational before/after attribution is association. Do not invent significance, sample thresholds or AI-derived uplift. Obtain qualified statistical review and verified configured analysis when a causal decision depends on it. No new stats engine is shipped.
8. Deliver one conditional recommendation with its reason, counterevidence, freshness/maturity limits and explicit missing proof. With no actual observations, report DESIGNED, NOT EXECUTED and the blocking evidence gap. A descriptive lift alone approves no price change. Independent construction Security then Quality and parent repository health remain required before any authorized commit.
