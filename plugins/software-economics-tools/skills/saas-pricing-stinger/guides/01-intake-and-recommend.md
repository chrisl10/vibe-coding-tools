# Prepare one initial pricing recommendation

Read [distillation](../references/research/distillation.md), [model selection](../references/model-selection.md) and [worksheet](../references/templates/pricing-worksheet.md). Ask one compact batch for customer/buyer, problem/outcome, substitutes, measurable value, intended market/currency, price unit, workload spread, support/AI/storage costs, payment/refund assumptions, fixed costs, owner-selected target margin and available customer evidence.

1. Distinguish the person using the product from the person paying. Identify a concrete job, alternative and budget process. Record evidence date, segment, observation and counterevidence. A generic competitor price is not proof that this buyer pays for this offer. SBA supports demand/alternative and direct-research questions. Source.
2. Resolve Competitive Research through trusted Core's selected-owner resolver or the source catalog. Request current segment-matched substitutes, units, limits, taxes/region/currency and feature differences. If no selected owner or reliable comparison exists, label market evidence missing rather than inventing a going rate.
3. Resolve Operating Cost for low/base/high workload rates and operational/support responsibilities. Resolve Unit Economics for expected contribution, gross margin and fixed-cost break-even using supplied price scenarios. The owner's existing calculator handles payments/refunds and cost classification; don't write another helper or quietly invent fees. Show missing inputs as unknown, never zero.
4. Select one price model and readable unit with [model-selection.md](../references/model-selection.md). Bound included users, actions/storage and support. Value metric means the thing the buyer believes is worth paying for; cost driver means the thing making delivery expensive. They may differ. Prefer a unit supported by both customer understanding and predictable economics.
5. Compare proposed prices against the owner-selected economic target and customer/substitute evidence. A cost floor is the amount needed for the modeled service to work financially; it does not show willingness to pay. A requested margin is a supplied business goal, not an industry standard. If unit contribution is negative, increasing customer count cannot cure that scenario. Ask owners to revise scope, limits, costs or price assumptions and retest.
6. Deliver one provisional initial price/model recommendation, dated evidence/counterevidence, scope/limits, buyer value reasoning, cost feasibility and the highest-impact uncertainty. With no market evidence, a supplied seed price may be a private test candidate, never a validated launch price. If no seed exists, recommend completing the price-discovery worksheet before selecting an amount.
7. Follow [experiment guide](02-test-and-review.md) and set owner-selected thresholds before contact. Provide reviewable artifacts only. Sending offers, taking payments, creating billing objects, changing public pricing and contacting customers require separate authorization and selected implementation owners.

Use [worked cases](../examples/pricing-cases.md) to keep maths and uncertainty understandable. An allowance, seat count, invoice, tax or legal commitment needs an accepted implementation contract outside this advisory.
