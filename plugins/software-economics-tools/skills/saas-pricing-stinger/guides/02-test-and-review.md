# Design and review a pricing experiment

Complete [experiment.md](../references/templates/experiment.md) before any external action. Record the hypothesis, segment/buyer, proposed offer with exact included limits, price unit/currency/period, intended evidence type, owner-selected sample/time/budget, success/stop thresholds and confounders. State that no research has run until actual observations exist. SBA distinguishes general data from direct customer insight. [Source](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/raw/sba-business.md).

Prepare neutral questions about the last time the buyer solved the problem, the substitute used, cost in time/money, procurement authority and what would make this offer unsuitable. Present a clearly identified proposal when price discussion is authorized; don't fabricate urgency, competitors or customer adoption. Declared willingness is interest evidence, not a paid transaction. Free usage, signups and search interest are weaker than an actual authorized purchase and continued use.

Choose one test suited to the biggest unknown: problem/value interviews when buyer need is unclear; a clearly scoped price/offer conversation when value is established; a separately authorized paid pilot when practical acceptance is the question. Predeclare what evidence would change the recommendation. Retain objections/refusals and competing explanations such as poor onboarding or missing capability; do not explain every refusal as price sensitivity.

Example-only thresholds: an owner might choose ten qualified buyer conversations, six matching documented problem histories, and three requests for a pilot proposal as a threshold to prepare the next test. These fictional thresholds are not scientific confidence or proof of demand. A paid-pilot stage might require two owner-authorized paying pilots and usage/retention evidence before a launch decision. No universal conversion rate is encoded.

Review economics at high usage and support load, check meter/customer comprehension, and preserve one currency/period across comparisons. Usage tiers and credit allowances need event definitions, caps, rounding, late/duplicate events, purchased versus promotional/included balances and bill-dispute handling in the implementation handoff. Never count one included credit twice. [Stripe credit context](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/software-economics-tools/skills/saas-pricing-stinger/references/research/raw/stripe-credits.md).

Write observations with dates, source/permission, outcomes and counterevidence. Recommend keep, revise or stop, with one reason and a next owner decision. Payments owns accepted billing implementation; legal specialists own assurance about refunds, prepaid credits, tax and contract terms. No publication, price write or customer send follows automatically from the advisory.
