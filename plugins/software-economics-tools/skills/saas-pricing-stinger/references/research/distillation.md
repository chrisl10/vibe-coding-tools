Inference and declared policy: cost feasibility does not demonstrate willingness to pay. A low-cost tool can still have no buyers; expensive delivery can prevent a viable offer even when customers like it. Keep evidence labels, counterevidence and experiment thresholds beside a provisional recommendation. Quantitative targets and sample thresholds must come from the owner, not a universal rule invented here.

Mathematics is delegated to Unit Economics, workload costs to Operating Cost, current substitute comparison to Competitive Research. This component does not reproduce those helpers. Examples are hypothetical supplied inputs and existing-owner calculator exercises. No customer interviews, purchases or live experiments have been performed.
