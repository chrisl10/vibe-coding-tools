# 06. Close out

1. Reconcile each PR, proof and deploy with current observed state. Mark local-only, queued, unproven and handed-off work honestly; a plan or chip offer is not shipment.
2. Save `references/templates/manager-handoff.md` in the local effort folder. Include owner decision sources, scoped delegation, CASE verdicts and conditions, KIPP cards/index, confirmed numbering, pending questions, owned worktrees and exact candidate proofs.
3. Never automatically archive CASE or KIPP. Leave them idle with their remaining work recorded. Archive finished work chips only under the owner's scoped authorization and the host's approval controls; no default permission comes from this skill.
4. `CronDelete` created ticks and `TaskStop` created poller/fallback jobs. Confirm timer and monitor stop state with the live tools. Do not modify unrelated watchers or sessions.
5. Append the ledger's final event and give the required closing report: status, delivered, verified, not done, owner questions and out-of-scope observations. Report actual checks and unfinished gates; no automatic commit, push, promotion or deployment follows close-out.
