# 05. Questions and decisions

Use [07-case-and-kipp.md](07-case-and-kipp.md) and the recorded owner profile for authority. Historical field notes do not grant a new effort any permissions.

1. Classify the question: routine TARS decision, CASE proportion or delegated decision, KIPP feature intent, or owner-only gate.
2. TARS answers routine questions within the assigned scope and records a ruling ID with UTC time, evidence and decision source.
3. For CASE, send one batch: actual owner ask, numbered questions, draft answer and lean, exact candidate/revision, reports and relevant paths. Record one verdict per question with inline conditions. The smallest option that meets the full ask wins only under the owner's tie-breaker delegation.
4. CASE PROCEED is advice unless the owner explicitly delegated that named decision to TARS plus CASE. Verify and cite the delegation before acting. Never infer permission to widen the task, amend Accepted contracts, spend, publish or promote.
5. Ask real owner gates in short voice-friendly questions, recommendation first. Use the live AskUserQuestion schema and actual capacity, with the profile's contact cadence. Tell the blocked chip what is waiting; continue independent authorized work.
6. Verified owner approvals carry across the named coordinated sessions within their specific scope. Relay the exact words, time and traceable source. Distinguish direct owner approval from a delegated decision. If the source is unclear, resolve it once before dependent work; do not demand repeated owner answers merely because a session changed.
7. KIPP records owner feature answers on cards and sends one routing handoff to TARS. TARS confirms numbering, takes new PRDs/contracts through CASE, then briefs Library. A feature card grants no implementation authority.
8. Batch messages by event and recipient. A refused or capped tool call is not permission to bypass controls. Never answer a question by editing the chip's product files yourself.
