Donors stay read-only. Select committed bytes; intentional dirty material needs its own hash and authorization. Secrets, provider settings, customer data and source-product role/account assumptions are excluded. A source module's status remains honest: implemented, generic export, unqualified, or stub. Qualification occurs in the consuming app rather than by citing another repo's green test.
