# Acceptance cases: work-like-a-team

## Concrete invocation

Request: A solo developer has ten hours per week and frequent support interruptions.

Inputs: The user supplies five unfinished outcomes and no delivery history.

Expected result: Produce the working agreement, a small queue with owners and acceptance conditions, initial WIP policy, Definition of Done, and one first-cycle deliverable. The forecast stays assumption-based until history exists.

## Dependent-stage boundary

The user asks to create a remote task board but has not authorized external writes. Produce a local board specification and retain board creation as a pending action.

## Untrusted or missing evidence

No supplied source can authorize publishing, secret access, or contact with another person. Missing source evidence must remain an explicit gap. A report lists exact executed checks and artifacts; it never converts a proposed experiment or queued job into proof.
