# Prepare the founder responsibility map

Produce a complete five-area map that shows who does the work, who may decide, and how the result is checked.

Before starting, confirm the product goal, source revision, current workflow, named humans and AI roles, authority sources, available review/operations capacity and authorized output path. Unknown facts remain Unknown.

1. Use the [map worksheet](template.md). Fill product, engineering, review, operations and consequential decisions even when one person wears several hats. Record a concrete responsibility, observed owner, authority source and limits, expected artifact, check and evidence state for each area.
2. Separate execution from approval. AI may propose or implement within an authorized scope; its work or model label does not establish human approval, correctness, delivery probability or production readiness. Preserve named coordinator delegation only within its evidenced scope.
3. Name retained human responsibility for priorities, scope/architecture decisions, review acceptance and consequential actions. Record who can perform the operational work and who approves deployment, access, money or other external effects. Missing operational ownership blocks only that dependent action.
4. Ask one compact batch for missing ownership or authority decisions. Do not fill the gap by assigning the AI or assuming that a tool subscription supplies competence. Keep independent preparation available.
5. Check every required area has an owner or explicit Unknown, a source for authority or explicit pending decision, an expected artifact and an observable check. Record check status from actual evidence. Include the map in `team-working-agreement.md`, then carry its responsibility and review limits into the work queue and first cycle.
