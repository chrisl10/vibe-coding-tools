# Evidence grades

Derived bookkeeping rubric from the distillation. Do not convert these labels into a probability or an automatic decision.

| Grade | Meaning | Claim limit |
|---|---|---|
| CONTEXT | Dated official/public market or competitor source | Describes context; does not prove this offer's demand. |
| REPORTED | Actual sourced participant/customer statement | Reports what was said, with selection/incentive limitations. |
| OBSERVED | Actual recorded behavior under the declared stimulus and conditions | Supports the observed behavior/proxy in that sample only. |
| HYPOTHESIS | Untested assumption, inferred explanation, or future prediction | Requires a test; never counts as an interview or observed result. |
| MISSING | Required observation/source unavailable | No result can be asserted for that criterion. |

Contradictory evidence uses the same grade system and remains visible. A source can be reliable yet irrelevant to the claimed segment. Source provenance and claim relevance are recorded separately.
