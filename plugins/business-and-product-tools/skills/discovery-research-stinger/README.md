# discovery-research-stinger

The continuous-discovery playbook for `discovery-research-wasp-drone`. This stinger encodes Teresa Torres' interview cadence, Opportunity Solution Trees, Jobs-to-be-Done interview methodology, DVFU assumption mapping, and lightweight prototype experiment design  -  giving the Angel specific, opinionated procedures rather than generic UX theory.

**Procedure:** [skill procedure](SKILL.md)
**Research summary:** [`research/research-summary.md`](research/research-summary.md)
**Primary source coverage:** Nov 2025 - May 2026 (13 research notes)

Start with `SKILL.md` for the procedure index, then read `guides/00-principles.md` to understand the philosophy before loading any domain guide.

## Demand and first-version decisions

The additive guides [07 demand test](guides/07-test-demand-before-building.md), [08 next decision](guides/08-continue-pivot-or-stop.md), and [09 first-version scope](guides/09-scope-first-version.md) connect a declared assumption to actual evidence and one scoped recommendation.
