## Thin evidence and conflicts

Five primary snapshots ground the comparison questions and one vendor counterexample. They do not constitute a full market sweep, actual supplier due diligence, a legal opinion, a priced quote or buyer-specific recommendation. Export help documents describe administrative exports; API terms describe API access. These are different routes and must not be substituted for one another. A documented feature, a contractual entitlement and an observed restoration are separate evidence rows.
