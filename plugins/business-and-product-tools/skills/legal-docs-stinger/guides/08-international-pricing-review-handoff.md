# Prepare international pricing for accountant and counsel review

This guide prepares the facts needed for Terms/MSA currency, tax and refund disclosures. Legal Docs owns the document handoff, not tax advice or accounting determinations. Read the research digest, then use the [packet](../templates/international-pricing-review-packet.md) and [cases](../examples/international-pricing-review-cases.md).

## Procedure

1. Ask one compact fact batch: seller establishment/registrations, buyer jurisdictions and B2B/B2C evidence, product/bundle, seller or marketplace role, sale volume/period, desired currency and inclusive/exclusive price. Preserve absent answers as Unknown.
2. Record separate legal classification, commercial assumptions and arithmetic. A customer's address or a vendor tax feature cannot establish registration, nexus, exemptions or tax liability. Name the reviewer for each unresolved determination.
3. Refresh authoritative guidance for the actual jurisdictions and transaction date. Capture URL, retrieval date, published/updated date, effective date where stated, exact supporting passage and applicability limit. Retrieval date is not legal effective date. UK and Canada examples are illustrative research anchors, not worldwide coverage.
4. Record exact account-country/provider contract terms for processing, international-card, FX, billing/invoicing, refunds and disputes. Headline rates are not an account quote. Retain charged-total versus net-sale fee bases explicitly.
5. Prepare accountant/tax-counsel questions for classification, location, nexus/registration, collection/remittance, invoice requirements and exemptions/withholding. Prepare legal-counsel questions for price disclosure, renewal, refunds and consumer rights. Do not resolve these through general privacy-regime matrices.
6. Resolve `unit-economics-stinger` or `saas-pricing-stinger` through trusted Core's selected-plugin resolver before a Software Economics handoff. Missing owner means a named availability gap. Reuse their supplied-rate worksheet. Their single-currency calculator does not model tax/FX; preserve an explicit transparent extension and exclusions instead of adding a duplicate calculator or forcing unsupported inputs.
7. Deliver the packet, assumption reconciliation, dated source ledger and unresolved decision register. Label numerical work as supplied-input scenario arithmetic. Leave dependent production pricing or tax setup pending the specific owner decision. Publishing, filing, accounts and provider writes require separate authorization.

## Completion check

Seller and buyer facts have supplied evidence or Unknown; inclusive/exclusive mode and fee bases are explicit; currencies are separated; source freshness is distinguishable from effective dates; no rate is applied without a supplied classification decision; refunds/invoices are reviewed; no legal or compliance certification appears. Unknown duty or treatment blocks a real gross-to-net recommendation, but a fictional sensitivity exercise may proceed with its assumptions labeled.

This is a generated draft for reference. Have a qualified attorney licensed in your jurisdiction review all legal documents before publishing or countersigning.
