## Derived operating method

Our method is an explicit synthesis, not a source-prescribed make/buy rule. Separate a persistent skill or ownership gap from a short peak in demand. Show time, review bottlenecks, cash and exit obligations together. Record a proposed recommendation and what evidence would reverse it. Derivation uses the four narrow claims above and user-supplied constraints; there is no salary, hiring lead-time, ramp, agency performance or AI speed benchmark in this archive.
