# Capacity planning

Prepare a local operational recommendation from demand, actual capacity and supplied service/hiring scenarios. This specialist owns the make/buy staffing decision worksheet; existing specialists own forecasting, flow, commercial quotations, payroll/classification and hiring systems.

## When to use

- Should I hire another developer or buy specialist services?
- Will more people help when review or support is the bottleneck?
- How do onboarding, coordination and continuing ownership change usable capacity?

## Procedure

1. Read [README](README.md), [measure capacity](guides/01-measure-capacity.md), [digest](references/research/distillation.md) and [model](references/model.md). Ask a compact batch for missing constraints.
2. Fill the [worksheet](references/templates/worksheet.md), separating actual evidence from assumptions and constructed scenarios. Preserve calendar, skill, review and support limits; deduct costs/time once.
3. Read [make/buy recommendation](guides/02-recommend-make-buy.md). Resolve selected Estimation, Kanban Flow, Services Quoting, HR Payroll and Hiring ATS owners for their dependent stages. Do not duplicate their professional decisions.
4. Check the [fictional example](examples/make-buy.md) and [negative cases](references/negative-cases.md). Deliver one provisional recommendation with reasons, deferred scope, evidence, dependencies, unknowns and reversal/calibration triggers.
