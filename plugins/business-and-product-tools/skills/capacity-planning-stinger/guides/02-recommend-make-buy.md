# Prepare one hire or service recommendation

Use the baseline from [measurement](01-measure-capacity.md), the [model](../references/model.md) and [worked example](../examples/make-buy.md). This is operational preparation, with professional and execution decisions handed off.

1. Compare the existing team with explicitly reduced scope, a calibrated hiring proposal and a bounded service proposal over the same horizon. Assess persistence of demand, specialist need, product knowledge, ownership and actual start availability. Never default to a permanent hire, offshore agency or particular vendor.
2. Keep proposed contribution separate from onboarding and current-team review/coordination/acceptance/support/exit load. Validate skills, dependencies and a retained accountable owner. GitLab's own process illustrates bounded need, scope and lifecycle, not a universal staffing rule. [GitLab archive](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/capacity-planning-stinger/references/research/raw/gitlab-contractors.json)
3. Record supplied dated quotes, exclusions and acceptance evidence. Supplier hours alone do not establish usable completed scope; specify deliverables, feedback and revision constraints. Resolve Services Quoting for commercial scope and cost interpretation. [Upwork archive](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/capacity-planning-stinger/references/research/raw/upwork-milestones.json)
4. State cash and time separately, with unpriced costs visible. Resolve HR Payroll for worker model/payroll and qualified legal/finance advice; Hiring ATS for recruitment pipeline. This skill does not determine classification, contract enforceability, salaries, employment terms or financing.
5. Deliver one provisional recommendation, the scope deferred, its dependencies and what would reverse it. For missing throughput or quote evidence, recommend a bounded evidence-gathering checkpoint instead of a confident hiring/buying conclusion. No hiring or procurement action occurs.
6. Agree on a later local review using actual completions, review/support hours and ownership issues. The date is a proposed checkpoint, not a scheduled job or external notification. Consult [negative cases](../references/negative-cases.md).
