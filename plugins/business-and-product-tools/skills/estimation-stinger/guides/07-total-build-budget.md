# Estimate total build cash including AI tools
Read [guide 06](06-ai-assisted-forecast.md), the [source digest](../references/build-budget/research/distillation.md), and [worksheet](../references/build-budget/worksheet.md). This existing pair owns O037; it adds no calculator or duplicate specialist.

1. Collect accepted first-version scope, experience, N daily hours, working days, readiness dependencies, comparable completed work, currency, build dates and funded operating horizon. Ask one compact batch for missing inputs. Count engineering, learning, review/verification and rework once each. Separate paid contractor tasks from owner effort and owner-time valuation. No AI multiplier converts generation speed into delivery certainty.
2. Use the unchanged forecast helper to show conditional working days and dates. Use explicit supplied zeros for its cash fields only when choosing **schedule-only mode**, naming the separate cash ledger in cost_basis and labeling direct_cash as excluded from this run. Unknown rates must never become zeros. If all inputs exist, its cash result may be shown as a separate illustrative 30-day-block estimate; never add it again to the invoice ledger.
3. Build a dated line-item ledger. Separate subscription seat charges, included quota, additional usage, API tokens by applicable input/cache/output meter, agent runtime, tools/search/storage, build infrastructure and minimum floors. Also include contractor/reviewer cash, licenses/assets/devices, setup, distribution/account fees, and tax/FX evidence or gaps. No vendor name supplies a price. Fresh official model/plan/region/contract rates and quantities are mandatory for each actual calculation. Verify quota units and reset dates; token estimates require representative measurement or explicit scenario assumptions.
4. Give each row a unique cost ID and nonoverlapping covered interval. Record billing cycle, invoice/due date, commitment period, cash paid versus economic allocation, scope and provenance. Annual subscription cash is the actual prepayment, while the build allocation is a separately labeled view; do not add the two. Existing sunk subscription expense may have zero incremental cash only with evidence, while its attributed use remains a separate allocation. A quota is a usage allowance, not spendable cash. [Primary invoice semantics](../references/build-budget/research/distillation.md).
5. Resolve Operating Cost through trusted Core's selected owner for applicable monthly arithmetic; use its existing model only when the invoice's fee/credit ordering and linear monthly units fit. Convert per-million tokens to a documented unit before input. Shared credit pools receive one global ID and reconcile opening balance, eligible build allocations, eligible operating allocations, other committed usage, expiration and remaining balance. Do not reset the same finite pool for every month or scenario period. Each scenario has its own hypothetical balance, not additive budgets. [Model](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/estimation-stinger/references/build-budget/research/raw/packs--software-economics-tools--skills--operating-cost-stinger--references--model.md).
6. Reconcile build cash, separately named contingency reserve and the first operating period. A delay changes service holding periods as applicable, not merely labor. The launch boundary cannot count a full build month and the same full operating month twice. Keep prepayment funding and usage cost separate. Ongoing operations remain Operating Cost's owner; commercial quote and selling-price decisions remain Services Quoting/Unit Economics's owners.
7. Deliver lower/base/upper **conditional** ranges, current evidence dates, time/cash views, credit ledger, missing evidence, calibration checkpoint and one recommendation. If any material rate, currency, quantity or period is unknown, show a known subtotal with excluded gaps; withhold a complete total. A spreadsheet total is not approval to spend, configure budgets, purchase subscriptions or commit to dates. Do not access invoices requiring credentials or perform provider writes. Retain the inputs, calculator output and reconciled worksheet in the agreed local advisory destination.

Check [fictional examples](../references/build-budget/fictional-examples.md) before delivery. Arithmetic proof here validates supplied scenarios only. Component promotion still requires independent parent Security, Quality and parent-run repository-health gates; installed-host availability remains separate.
