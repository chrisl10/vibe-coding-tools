# License evidence digest
Retrieved 2026-10-07. These are educational summaries of the named text, not decisions about a particular combined work. Exact license version, exception and offered alternative govern the review.

| Text | Evidence-supported reading | Locator |
|---|---|---|
| OSI definition | Source visibility alone is insufficient; the definition allows commercial endeavors and redistribution under its conditions. | OSD, introduction, criteria 1, 6 |
| MIT | Permission to use, change and sell is conditioned on retaining copyright and permission notices in copies or substantial portions. Warranty is disclaimed. | MIT, permission paragraph and following condition |
| Apache 2.0 | Redistribution includes license-copy, changed-file notices, retained relevant notices and applicable NOTICE attribution. Patent grant and termination have their own terms; trademark rights are not a blanket grant. | Apache, sections 3, 4, 6 |
| GPLv3 | Private covered work that is not conveyed has a different boundary from conveying source or object copies. Mere network interaction without transferring a copy is not conveying. Object-code conveyance and corresponding-source methods require section 6 review. | GPL, sections 0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 |
| AGPLv3 | Section 13 addresses a modified Program whose version supports remote network interaction: interacting users must be prominently offered its Corresponding Source. Determining the covered work needs evidence; it does not automatically classify every server or adjacent service. | AGPL, sections 1, 2, 13 |
| FSF FAQ | The licensor's explanations help interpret private use, selling, distribution and combinations. They do not replace the exact license text or determine this user's disputed copyright boundaries. | FAQ, anchors GPLRequireSourcePostedPublic, DoesTheGPLAllowMoney, GPLInProprietarySystem |
| SPDX | Standard identifiers, text and exceptions support identification. Identifying a label does not prove the right to grant that license or compatibility of a combined artifact. | SPDX, introduction; final sentence is an evidence limit inferred from identification scope |

Claims about font, model, dataset or content permissions are deliberately unassessed by these code-license sources. Inventory separate artifacts and their actual terms. Unknown ownership, linking boundary or authorization remains Unknown. Do not infer rights from a repository badge, scanner, hash, pricing page or code availability.
