# Review the boundary with evidence
1. Pin exact component/version/license expression and original license text, exceptions, notices, modifications and product artifacts. Distinguish original licensor terms from an author's unsupported description. Hashes bind evidence bytes; they do not prove license compatibility. [SPDX](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/software-licensing-stinger/references/research/raw/spdx.txt), introduction.
2. Draw separate boundaries for private internal use, customer-delivered JavaScript/browser bundles, desktop/mobile packages, container delivery and remotely used server work. Record who receives a copy, what files it contains and who interacts remotely. Source map or compiled output is still evidence to inventory; do not assume all customer code is only on a server.
3. For GPLv3, locate the actual conveyed covered material and proposed corresponding-source path under sections 4 through 6. Mere network interaction without copy transfer is not conveying; delivered clients remain a separate review. Private-use discussion cannot decide combinations or contractor arrangements. [GPL](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/software-licensing-stinger/references/research/raw/gpl.txt), sections 0, 2, 4, 5, 6.
4. For AGPLv3, determine whether the Program was modified and supports remote network interaction, then record the section 13 offer and Corresponding Source proposal for those users. Do not claim all SaaS is exempt or every unrelated service is covered. [AGPL](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/software-licensing-stinger/references/research/raw/agpl.txt), sections 1 and 13.
5. Ask the authorized reviewer to determine uncertain covered-work, linkage, aggregation, exception, alternative-license and source-scope issues from real evidence. Do not resolve them by process boundaries or repository layout alone. [FSF guidance](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/software-licensing-stinger/references/research/raw/fsf-faq.txt), GPLInProprietarySystem; license text controls.
6. Return a draft obligation inventory and exact unresolved questions. Do not change files, remove notices, publish source, grant a license, contact upstream or declare compliance. Legal counsel resolves legal compatibility; vendor-maintenance owns authorized vendor edits, Dependency Audit owns graph/SBOM evidence, Legal Docs owns customer legal documents.
