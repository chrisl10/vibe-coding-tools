# Prepare one commercial distribution proposal
1. Capture the intended sale, hosting or delivery, recipients, exact artifact, release identity, modifications and owner. Commercial and open-source are not opposites: inspect the named license's conditions alongside the revenue plan. [OSD](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/software-licensing-stinger/references/research/raw/osi-osd.txt), criteria 1, 6; [GPL](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/software-licensing-stinger/references/research/raw/gpl.txt), section 4 permits a price for conveyed copies.
2. Complete the inventory template, including third-party content and separate service terms. Unknown ownership is not cured by buying a repository or changing its name. Require the controlling user's authorization and evidence of the actual rights held before proposing changes to licensing or distribution.
3. Prepare notice/license/source-offer review items tied to each shipped component, exact license version and relevant clauses. Preserve exceptions and dual-license alternatives as offered by the actual rights holder, never invented by the agent. [MIT](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/software-licensing-stinger/references/research/raw/mit.txt), condition; [Apache](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/software-licensing-stinger/references/research/raw/apache.txt), section 4; [GPL](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/software-licensing-stinger/references/research/raw/gpl.txt), sections 5, 6; [AGPL](https://github.com/legioncodeinc/the-wasp-nest-source/blob/main/packs/business-and-product-tools/skills/software-licensing-stinger/references/research/raw/agpl.txt), section 13.
4. Record the authorized decision maker, counsel review needed, proposed changes, affected artifacts and reversal evidence. A recommendation to obtain a missing license file is allowed. Final legal compatibility, license selection, acceptance, provider purchases, relicensing and publication require their selected owners and explicit authority.
5. Return advisory text through the parent for the agreed local report destination. No outbound action or filesystem changes by this read-only specialist. Separate static source validation from actual shipped artifacts, native agent dispatch, legal review and customer outcomes.
